# Ring Box Studio — whole-repo audit, 2026-09-10

Four read-only reviews run in parallel after the production plate was written:
optics and math correctness, backend maintainability, frontend and renderer,
product and feature gaps. Findings are ranked; items marked **fixed today** were
corrected in the same session (commits after `d987005`).

## 1. Math and physics

### Wrong, and it changed the mask — fixed today
1. **The frame finish erased the two finest leaf families.** `witness_dies.FINISH_FRAME_UM`
   was 1.2 µm; an open of radius r deletes every line under 2r = 2.4 µm, and the
   single-ply garland's 4.15 and 4.47 µm periods write 2.08 and 2.24 µm lines. On
   the 07:37 plate both families were bare glass on every photo side (3 and 0
   stripes against thousands of the coarser rungs), and the DRC was clean
   because nothing was left to flag. The guard compared against the 2 µm litho
   floor, not the finish radius, and probed only the first family. Fixed:
   radius 1.0 µm on both zones, guard over every family against 2 × finish;
   survival verified line by line (beach: 344/349 at 4.15, 3189/3205 at 4.47).
2. **Halftone quantiser lost half a level everywhere.** `screenrects.py` used
   `floor(tone·steps)`; measured −0.50 level (−2.3 points of coverage) on a
   uniform field. Fixed: round-to-nearest. The A1 gate's residual bias was
   attributed to the finish in the docs; the finish contributes +0.1, the
   quantiser −0.5.
3. **`seam_list` overstated the two side bottom seams** by 2t (32 vs 27.5 mm):
   the left and right walls sit between front and back. Fixed in backend and
   frontend, golden fixture regenerated.

### Wrong in the documents — fixed today
4. **Flash geometry.** With the lamp behind the viewer the first order returns
   at sin θ = λ/2p (Littrow), not λ/p: 2.6° to 3.8° across the ladder, not 5.2°
   to 7.6°. And with every family at one orientation the families do not "come
   up one after another": at one tilt all six light at once in different colours
   (at 3.0°: 434 nm at 4.15 µm to 630 nm at 6.02 µm) and rocking the box sweeps
   the hues. The tolerable lamp width for saturated colour is ~5° retro, twice
   the normal-incidence figure.
5. **Contrast figures are transmission numbers.** The box is seen in reflection
   off gold, where V_R = V_T · T̄/(1−T̄) = V_T/3 for two 50% gratings: the
   garland's surviving 0.42 becomes 0.14. Conclusions hold; numbers relabelled.
6. **The specular double pass is not modelled.** In reflection with the lamp
   near the eye the ray crosses the front chrome twice: t_F(x)·t_B(x+s)·t_F(x+2s).
   For a point source, at the 2.51° swap 2s = p/2 and the front comb extinguishes
   itself; the monogram would pulse every 1.27°. A lamp ≳ 2–3° wide averages it
   away. Together with item 4 this is a two-sided lamp requirement (≳2–3°,
   ≲5°), now stated in the physics doc (§2.4b, §2.5b).

### Stated approximations whose size was not
7. Shader inner-uv shift is paraxial (T/n)·tan θ: exact at 2.5°, +8.5% at 30°,
   +24% at 45°, +61% at 60°. Fine for the design angles; the far orbits over-shift.
8. Coloured halftone bands cost 0.5 of the metal in area but 0.25 in the
   specular direction (η₀ = c²): a full stop darker under a lamp, not half.
9. The near-field monogram window holds 1.75 beat periods; the 48.1% figure has
   several-percent methodological uncertainty.
10. `sim2d.switch_metrics` phases channels from the raster's left edge, not the
    face centre; `validate_dies` compensates with a phase search, other callers
    would not. Its default `shift_um = p/2` is the blend point, a trap.

### Verified correct
Beat delta, parallax 26.93 µm/°, Fresnel number and p_min 41.2 µm, swap angle
2.511° for the 270.5 µm comb, eye pitch 65.5 µm, gap-scaled comb, monogram beat
1635 µm, barrier registration end to end (phase −0.25 slit over the A|B boundary,
±p/4 reveals, p aliases), exact-area rasteriser (0.50000 at 0° and 45°), the
1−(1−A)(1−B) union, (1−R)² on both buried layers, bonded cut dims, stepped-edge
foil (3p), art rim 3.64 mm, both-ply mirroring for the chrome-down stack. Ring
clearance is exactly 0.0 mm on all three axes: with ±50 µm dicing tolerance a
0.5 mm margin is advisable (Jay's call; it changes the box).

## 2. Backend maintainability

Fixed today: box manifest written without the cache lock and non-atomically
(`materialize_box`, `get_box` now go through `cache_lock`, `write_json_atomic`,
`read_json_cache`); plate layout overflow computed but never enforced (now
raises); `klayout` and `scikit-image` undeclared (resolved through gdsfactory);
two test files in no chunk (`test_export_blank`, `test_export_svg_rects`, now in
the witness chunk); CLAUDE.md/README said five chunks (ten).

Ranked backlog:
1. **One face taxonomy.** The blank / photo / interlace / scanimation / single-ply
   dispatch is recomputed in six places (`_carrier_recipe_data`,
   `_raster_compose_plate`, `_bake_plate_svg`, `build_plate_fine`, zone masks,
   `witness_dies`). Today's "fab SVG still drew a carrier" bug and the eight
   version bumps are symptoms. A `FaceKind` computed once from `PlateSpec`. (M)
2. **`app/production.py`.** ~40 production constants across seven modules; the
   2 µm floor is declared three times under three names, DBU twice, the layer
   map four times. (M)
3. **Content fingerprint in the cache key** (constants + asset bytes + generator
   source) instead of hand-bumped versions; the `paris.png` edit needed a manual
   bump and five compose versions shipped with a manifest disagreeing with the
   gold. (M)
4. **Generate the TS production constants from Python** (or serve
   `GET /boxes/default`); today a dial change is a six-file edit with literals
   pinned on both sides. The assembly golden triangle is the right pattern. (S–M)
5. Split `plates.py` (3336 lines, 49% prose, 138 lines of changelog) into spec /
   recipe / compose / literal / svg / photo. (M–L)
6. Loggers in the fab path (nine modules print, none log); `timing_s` into plate
   and box manifests. (S)
7. Test fixtures: four full-scale renders in chunk 1 need `extent_um`;
   `clear_field` options, `leaf_fills` non-hue fills and the packer's overflow
   branch are untested; assert against constants, not literals. (S–M)
8. `just` recipes for `gen_assembly_golden`, `validate_dies`, the GDS probes;
   retire `write_verdicts.py`, `shot_server.py`. (S)
9. `encoding="utf-8"` on every text write (latent cp1252 hazard). (S)
10. Photo assets: 17 MB of personal photographs committed, re-cropped twice as
    new blobs; decide on LFS, derived-only assets, or an external directory. (S)

## 3. Frontend and renderer
- Recipes 0/1 (`runMoireInteractive`, `runStereoLenticular`) and their uniforms
  are unreachable; every face is `foliage_moire` and literal. Delete or gate. (M)
- No lint, format or CI. (M)
- Frame controls hide the dials the production faces use (`motif_scale`,
  `edge_gradient`, `understory`, `border_vine`, `corner_fans`); size and pitch
  presets have no entry for the production box, so it renders as "custom". (M)
- `defaultBoxSpec` mirrored by hand (see backend item 4). (M)
- Effects gates pixel-check only the front face; single-ply sheen and blank faces
  have no pixel assertion. (M)
- `parallax.glsl` docstring says "add", both callers subtract (consistent,
  comment wrong). 8×8 supersampling worth profiling on this host. Tooltips via
  `title` only. `tsc` clean, 111 unit tests green.
