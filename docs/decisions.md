# Decisions — quick reference

The numbers a change to the box touches most often, pulled into one place so
they stop drifting across documents. Two sources feed this file:

- **`docs/plan.md` §0** — the decision table (why each number is what it is,
  what it replaced, what verified it). This file reproduces that table
  verbatim; `plan.md` is still where the reasoning and the full prose live.
- **`docs/BOM.md`** — vendor/price numbers for the physical build (substrate,
  bonding adhesive, hinge stock, foil tape, cleaving tools). **BOM.md's
  prices and vendors are the record of the 2026-08-29 research run, not a
  second definition of the design numbers** — where a design number (tape
  width, glass thickness, ply count) appears in both places, this file and
  `plan.md` are the source; BOM.md should only ever be read for "what to buy
  and from whom."

## Current build, at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Glass** | 2.25 mm fused quartz, n = 1.4585 (5″ × 0.090″ chrome + resist mask blank stock) |
| **Wall** | 4.5 mm — a bonded PAIR of plies per face, physically (mechanical thickness / rigidity), but **optically single-layer**: only the OUTER ply of each face is written, the inner ply is bare glass off a second, uncoated blank. There is no interlayer optical effect (no carrier, no comb, no beat) anywhere on the box — see decision 10. |
| **Box** | 32 × 32 × 35 mm, sized by the ring envelope (`boxes.RING_*`) |
| **Carrier pitch** | **none on the box.** (65.5 µm was the eye-sized two-ply carrier pitch before decision 10; kept only in the hidden two-ply exemplars.) |
| **Foil tape** | **3/8″ copper (9525 µm)**, `boxes.PRODUCTION_TAPE_UM` — the 2.25 mm bonded stepped edge needs 3 plies of width (6.75 mm) to leave a fold; 1/4″ is 0.4 mm short and leaves none. `validate_bonded_assembly` rejects narrower tape. |
| **Dicing** | cut on a saw as a **dicing grid** (`export_witness.layout`): rows of one height, straight 1 mm streets, cut list in the manifest (`layout.dicing`), `DICING.md` beside the mask, GDS layer 2/0, saw-lane marks in chrome at street ends. Never a packer. |
| **Polarity** | CLEAR (darkfield mask, positive resist): exposed = etched = clear |
| **Centrepieces** | single-layer diffraction mappings by region (`app/region_art.py`) — `monogram-jp` (lid), `globe-atlantic` (front) |

## Decision table (from `docs/plan.md` §0)

| # | Decision | Taken |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Blank** | 5″ × 0.090″ fused-quartz mask blank, 2.25 mm, chrome + resist. The dies ARE box plies, so the box is built from 2.25 mm quartz plies (wall 4.5 mm) and every witness number is the box's. The glass lives in ONE place, `witness_geom.PLY_UM / GLASS_N`; comb, parallax rate, near-field limit and swap angles derive from it and a test pins them to the plate compositor. |
| 1b | **Box size** | **32 × 32 × 35 mm**, sized by the RING (`boxes.RING_*`): a 21 mm outer-diameter band standing 24 mm tall in a 1 mm liner over a 2 mm base pad needs a 23 × 23 × 26 mm interior, which is what 4.5 mm walls leave. The old 29.1 mm box was the largest whose twelve plies packed one blank; its 20.1 mm interior held no adult ring. |
| 1c | **Carrier pitch (retired with decision 10)** | **65.5 µm**, sized by the eye (`witness_geom.CARRIER_ARCMIN` = 0.75′ at 300 mm): the lines are invisible in hand, only the beat shows. Gap-scaling the 22 µm design pitch gave 99 µm — 1.13′, a hatch the eye resolves, which read as chunks. Cost, from the near-field ladder: the fringes keep 51% of zero-gap contrast across the ply at 65.5 µm (75% at 99, 34% at 55, 7% at 44). Leaf 71.4 µm, monogram 68.23 µm (beat 1635), comb 270.5 unchanged. Superseded on the box by decision 10 (no carrier at all); survives only in the hidden two-ply exemplars. |
| 2 | **Polarity** | CLEAR, as the whole plate. Pre-plated chrome, positive resist: exposed = etched = clear. Dies are inverted inside their own rectangle by one klayout Region boolean (`witness_dies.clear_field`) and decomposed to trapezoids; never the GEOS inverter. |
| 3 | **Faces** | top monogram-jp + garland, front globe (`globe-atlantic` since decision 10) + garland, and **all six** prepared photographs as side dies (`witness_dies.SIDE_PHOTOS`, every one with its authored colour plan): beach and sunset are the box's left and right and carry the box's own garland seeds 104 / 105; garden, paris, night-group and porch-group ride along as candidates with seeds 106–109. Spares: `SPARE_FACES` (a second lid, a second front rotated 90°) and `SPARE_SIDES` (a second beach, seed 110) fill the dicing rows. Every die is a face's own `PlateSpec` from `boxes.default_box_spec` through `export_fine.build_plate_fine` — one authoring path, the one the box bake uses. Every inner ply is bare glass. |
| 4 | **Gap-scaled pitches (first build, superseded)** | The first build ran `plates._carrier_recipe_data` at `carrier_scale_mode="gap"`: at t/n = 1543 µm (4.50× the 500 µm design point) the frame pair was 99 / 107.9 µm, the monogram carrier 105.38 µm (beat 1635 µm), the barrier comb 270.5 µm — confirmed by the near-field gate (75% of fringe contrast survives vs 11% at 22 µm). Decision 1c replaced this with the eye-sized `fixed` carrier (65.5 µm); the comb was unaffected. Both are moot after decision 10. |
| 5 | **Capybara** | Cut from the box (back and bottom are `blank`: bare quartz on both plies). Its 15 µm slots sit at Fresnel N = 0.07 on this glass. The P-SCAN ladder left the plate with it. |
| 5a | **Die finish radius** | `witness_dies.FINISH_FRAME_UM` = **1.0 µm**, the same as the art zone. An open of radius r erases every line under 2r; the 1.2 µm radius chosen when the frame held 36 µm leaf lines erased the 4.15 and 4.47 µm leaf families (2.08 / 2.24 µm lines) outright on the 07:37 plate — two of six families as bare glass on every photo side, invisible to the DRC because nothing was left to flag. The guard in `export_fine` now probes every family's line against the finish radius. Halftone quantiser fixed at the same time: `floor` lost half a level everywhere (−2.3 points of coverage); round-to-nearest. |
| 5b | **Single-ply sides** | photo + leaf frame on bare glass, **no carrier**. A carrier on the same ply as the leaves is a static union moiré: at the two-ply 2.5° offset its bright fringes were 1.5 mm apart and 0.4 mm wide, a few solid diagonal bars across every photo frame (the 2 µm heal welded them solid). One sheet cannot make a moiré that moves, so Jay chose the honest version: the picture dissolves to glass (`photo.CARRIER_COV` = 0) inside a garland whose leaves are **fine 50% diffractive gratings, one PERIOD per motif family** from the colour ladder 4.15–6.02 µm (`plates.SINGLE_PLY_LEAF_FILL = "hue"`, `SINGLE_PLY_LEAF_HUE_PERIODS_UM`, `leaf_fills.py`; the frame's angle buckets pick the family). Zero order is flat gold; with the lamp behind the viewer (retro-reflection, Littrow) the first order returns at sin θ = λ/2p — 2.6° to 3.8° of tilt at green across the ladder — so at one tilt every family lights at once, each in its own colour (at 3.0° the ladder spans 434 to 630 nm, violet to red), and rocking the box sweeps the hues through the families: the photographs' colour-zone physics applied to foliage, verified by M-CD, M-DUTY and D-PER on this plate. Lines 2.1–3 µm, under 0.05′: the leaves read as smooth gold. Chosen over angled lines at a common pitch because axis-aligned stripes leave rectangular gaps: 13× faster to finish, half the polygons and zero DRC flags per side (angled 10 µm lines: 98 s, 195k polygons, 506 flags; 6 µm: 324 s, 293k, 3462). See `docs/physics-appendix.md` for the physics. |
| 6 | **Experiments** | cut to the single-layer bench essentials for THIS process (8 cells): M-POL, M-CD dense/iso, M-DUTY 10/5, D-PER, WEDGE 44, H-ACU. The two-layer cells went with the bond (decision 11); the dropped ladders are in git history (0799344). |
| 7 | **Foil** | **3/8″ copper (9525 µm)**, `boxes.PRODUCTION_TAPE_UM`. The bonded stack wraps a 3-ply stepped edge, 6.75 mm; 1/4″ tape is 0.4 mm short of it and left no fold at all — the seam had no lap, the hinge nothing to solder to, and `vernier_blocks` (which needs a fold ≥ 0.48 mm to live in) wrote **no verniers**. `validate_bonded_assembly` now rejects tape narrower than 3 plies. Fold 1.39 mm per face. |
| 8 | **Art rim** | Front art starts at `assembly.bonded_art_keepout_um` = ply + fold = **3.64 mm** from the outer edge, not at the 1.89 mm foil rim: between the two the outer ply sits over the tape on the ledge and the inner ply's interior fold, with no back grating behind it, so a garland there was leaves on copper (52% of the old ring). Same rim on the single-ply sides so all six borders start on one line. Band **2.4 mm** on every face (`PRODUCTION_BAND_UM`), foliage at `motif_scale` **0.68**. Art boxes on the 32 mm box: lid 17.1 mm, front 15.8 mm, sides 13.3 mm. |
| 9 | **Written-data DRC** | `witness_dies.clear_field` opens the metal and then the CLEAR complement to the 2 µm floor (1.0 µm radius in the art box, 1.2 µm in the frame where the single-ply garland's two gratings cross at 2.5°) and settles whatever the shop-style width/space check still flags; convex decomposition (`PO_htrapezoids`), whose cut-point rounding leaves none of the notches the plain trapezoid one did. Every die's manifest carries `drc_written_front/back` — the check on the polygons actually in the file. |
| 10 | **Single-layer everything (2026-09-15)** | The first plate's bonded pairs were cleaved by hand and broke, and on glass Jay judged the two-ply moirés — the monogram's shading moiré and the globe's barrier switch — terrible. **Every face is now ONE written ply** (`boxes._SINGLE_PLY_FACES` = all six; the inner plies are bare quartz off the second blank, so the box's 4.5 mm walls and ring fit are unchanged). The centrepieces are **single-layer diffraction mappings** (`app/region_art.py`): the motif is a map of REGIONS, each written as a vertical 50 % grating at its own period from the colour ladder (4.15–6.02 µm) so it flashes its own colour under a lamp, or as solid gold; the photo colour-zone physics Jay liked, applied to the lid and the front. `monogram-jp` colours the J and the P (and their interlock) separately; the front is the new `globe-atlantic` — ONE orthographic view over the Atlantic holding the US with California, Colombia and Europe, land and countries coloured by region, graticule, limb and the two stars solid. No carrier, no comb, no beat anywhere on the box; the leaves stay per-family gratings (5b). Every photograph carries an **authored colour plan** (`colour_mode "authored"`, `assets/photos/<image>.colour.json`). |
| 11 | **Dicing grid** | The plate is cut on a saw, so `export_witness.layout` is a **row grid** with straight full-width streets, not a packer: rows of one height (32 mm: lid, lid spare, the front spare rotated 90°; two 30.5 mm rows: front + seven side spots; 6 and 5 mm bench rows), 1 mm streets, cut list in the manifest (`layout.dicing`), `DICING.md` beside the mask, red dashes on the map, GDS layer 2/0. Protocol: horizontal streets → strips; per strip, vertical streets → dies; a column piece's own cuts last. Vernier combs and the two-layer experiments (M-VERN, P-RULE, B-MOVE, NF, SWAP) are gone with the bond. |

(Decision numbering matches `docs/plan.md` §0, including its out-of-numeric-order placement of 9 after 8 and before 10 — preserved here rather than renumbered, so a reference to "decision N" means the same thing in both files.)

## Substrate / bonding / hinge / tape — vendor record
