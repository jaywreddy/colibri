## Confirmed defects — HIGH

### H1. 9 of 17 catalog patterns compose a silently blank centerpiece on box faces — preview AND fab
`backend/app/plates.py:699` (`_centerpiece_masks`), `frontend/src/ui/FaceEditor.tsx:99`

`_raster_compose_plate` never uses a pattern's own `generate()` output for the
centerpiece; it goes through `_centerpiece_masks`, a hardcoded if/elif ladder
covering only 8 slugs (colibri-flap-phase, globe-duo-phase, gear-quill-switch,
food-pair-chirp, capybara-scanimation, jamon-tray, monogram-jp,
inscription-line). Every other slug falls through to `return None` and
`_paste_centerpiece` paints nothing. The identical gap exists on the fab path
(`_center_masks` → same function), so the fabricated plate would ship with a
foliage frame and **no artwork**. Orphaned slugs: colibri-globe-lenticular,
colibri-globe-moire, emerald-facet-moire, orchid-shimmer-moire,
jp-monogram-phase, wayuu-kanasu-moire, globe-rotation-stereo,
monogram-carrier-reveal, bitmap-halftone. The picker thumbnail for these
comes from the *standalone* materialize path, so it looks correct — the user
picks an attractive tile and gets a blank box face with no error.

**Fix:** either add the missing 9 to `_centerpiece_masks` with real silhouette
art, or mark centerpiece capability on the Pattern class (e.g.
`has_centerpiece: ClassVar[bool]`), filter the FaceEditor picker on it, and
make `materialize_plate` raise a descriptive error for centerpiece-less slugs
instead of silently compositing an empty window.

### H2. Fab export can ship stale plate geometry after a `PLATE_COMPOSE_VERSION` bump
`backend/app/api/export.py:837` (`_resolve_box_faces`)

The export path decides "plate cached" with a bare
`manifest.json exists` check — it never compares the manifest's
`compose_version` against `plates.PLATE_COMPOSE_VERSION`, unlike
`_materialize_plate_locked` which treats a mismatch as a miss. Since plate
hashes cover spec only, a saved box's plate dirs keep their names across a
version bump, and export zips the old front/back SVG+PNG with **no recompose
and no warning** — while `fine.gds` is rebuilt fresh, so the bundle can carry a
fine mask and preview/SVG from *different geometry versions*: a direct
violation of the fab-SVG = preview-PNG contract. The App.tsx export staleness
gate doesn't help: right after `loadPreset` both spec keys come from the same
object and compare equal.

**Fix:** in `_resolve_box_faces`, read the cached manifest and require
`compose_version == PLATE_COMPOSE_VERSION` (same test as
`_materialize_plate_locked`) — or simply always call `materialize_plate(spec)`,
which is cheap on a true hit. A stale version must route through
`_rebuild_face_plate` exactly like a missing dir.

### H3. Corner blobs + vertical-seam end caps ignore glass thickness — the four top blobs engulf and protrude ~1 mm above the closed lid
`frontend/src/scene/BoxScene.tsx:1128`, `frontend/src/scene/solderBead.ts:103`

`makeCornerBlob(beadR * 1.35)` plus ±18 % lumps → up to 1.59 mm radius at
default bead 2 mm, planted at the top wall corners (y = 19.5 on a 40 mm box).
Blob top = 21.09 mm vs lid outer surface 20.0 mm; the vertical beads' spherical
caps also overshoot the rim by a full radius. Visually the lid reads soldered
shut at all four corners — the one thing the hinge exists to disprove. Bottom
blobs likewise hang through the ground-shadow plane.

**Fix:** shorten vertical beads so the cap apex lands at the rim (`length -=
2*beadR` or a `flatEnds` option), cap top-corner blob radius against lid
clearance, and distinguish bottom corners (pooled junction, keep blob) from
top corners (bead termination, small cap).

### H4. Inner gold plane renders pure black from inside the open box
`frontend/src/shaders/plate.frag:764`, material is `THREE.DoubleSide`

Back-facing pixels get `ndv` clamped to 0 → Schlick = 1.0 exactly →
`layerT = 0` multiplies the whole inner-plane radiance to zero (still writes
coverage alpha, so it occludes as a black silhouette), and the outer plane's
`fresnelGain` saturates to `1/GOLD_F0`. Looking into the open box is a
documented use case ("from inside it reads as genuine second-surface art") and
the @effects suite only orbits outside, so this is uncaught.

**Fix:** flip the shading basis on back faces — after normalizing
`normalWorld`, `float facing = gl_FrontFacing ? 1.0 : -1.0; normalWorld *=
facing;` and rebuild the bitangent from the flipped normal so the basis stays
right-handed.

### H5. Second-surface transmission double-counted and evaluated at the wrong angle
`frontend/src/shaders/plate.frag:800`

(1) three.js's transmission path already applies `(1-F)` when compositing the
inner plane through the slab; the shader multiplies another `(1-Rq)²` on top —
`(1-R)³` total instead of the physical `(1-R)²` (~15 % too dark at a 70°
orbit, a view-dependent error in exactly the recession-cue regime). (2) The
entry-interface term uses the *view* angle; it must be the *light* angle —
reciprocity equates reversed rays at the same angle, it doesn't transfer the
exit angle to the entry interface.

**Fix:** replace with a single entry transmission at the light angle
(`Rl = r0 + (1-r0)(1-ndl)⁵`; `layerT = isBack ? (1-Rl) : 1.0`) and let the
slab own the exit interface. Re-baseline @effects in the same change.

## Confirmed defects — MEDIUM

### M1. Solder beads centred on the joint line with a full-circular section — half of every bead is inside the glass
`frontend/src/scene/BoxScene.tsx:1115`, `solderBead.ts:110`

The bead axis sits exactly on the seam polyline (the plate surface) with a
360° tube. At defaults (beadR 1.0 mm, glass 0.5 mm) bottom-seam beads pass
clean through the wall and protrude 0.5 mm into the interior; corner beads
exit inside the box. With transmission 0.94 the submerged solder renders as a
dark bar floating inside every plate. **Fix:** add an `outward` unit bisector
to `SeamSegment` (both sides of the assembly contract + regen golden fixture)
and offset the bead — or properly, give the section flat wetted faces (see
roadmap R1).

### M2. Bead vertex normals hand-written and wrong (worst on end caps)
`frontend/src/scene/solderBead.ts:131` — caps ~40° off at the pole, the
asymmetry term lacks its dr/dθ contribution, and the undulation produces zero
shading response. **Fix:** delete the analytic normals, call
`geo.computeVertexNormals()` (exactly what `makeCornerBlob` already does).

### M3. Bead UV v-coordinate clamped to ≥ 1 — solder maps smear into stripes over the first ~1.2 mm of every bead
`frontend/src/scene/solderBead.ts:138` — `Math.max(1, …)` is correct for the
tile *count*, wrong for a *coordinate*. **Fix:** drop the clamp;
RepeatWrapping handles the rest.

### M4. Tin rims and lid edge boxes centred on shared surfaces — z-fighting geometry, 0.15 mm inside the closed lid
`frontend/src/scene/BoxScene.tsx:1140` — wall rims poke into the lid slab; lid
edge boxes share coincident planes with the lid faces (masked at defaults by
the foil strips, exposed at user-reachable tape/glass settings) and
interpenetrate at corners. **Fix:** one-sided coatings offset outside the
plate surfaces; trim corner overlap (see roadmap R8 for the proper shell
rebuild).

### M5. Specular Fresnel evaluated at N·V instead of V·H
`frontend/src/shaders/plate.frag:822` — the gold highlight whitens on the
wrong angle. **Fix:** compute `Fspec` from `dot(viewTangent, h)` and use it
only in the specular term; keep the N·V form for the body-reflectance gain.

### M6. Diffraction accent disperses ~9× too slowly for the 4.4 µm grating it stands in for
`frontend/src/shaders/plate.frag:413` — full hue cycle over 0.625 in
sin-space vs the physical 0.068; envelope repeats indefinitely with tilt; the
light direction never enters. **Fix:** use the grating equation (`λ = d·(sin
θ_out − sin θ_in)` along the 45° grating vector, d bound as a uniform from
plates.py), gate to 380–700 nm, map λ→sRGB with a CIE fit. Minimum stopgap:
coefficient 1.6 → ~14.7 and drop the spatial hue term.

### M7. Preview water crests drawn ~1.9× wider than the fab mask bakes
`frontend/src/shaders/plate.frag:512` — `crestHalf = 0.30*amp` vs backend
`0.16*amp` (capybara_scanimation.py:268); the justifying comment
misattributes the difference to `_floor_crest_thickness`, which is a sliver
remover that never narrows crests. **Fix:** match 0.16, or export the
constant into `recipe_data` and bind as a uniform so the pair cannot drift.

### M8. New export job silently deletes the previous job's finished-but-undownloaded archive
`backend/app/export_job.py:427` — `start_job` reaps all dead jobs and
`_sweep_stale_dirs(keep={new})` rmtree's a `done` job whose fab.zip was never
fetched; a second tab/bench script then 404s on a job the UI just reported
done. **Fix:** don't sweep `done`-and-not-yet-downloaded jobs (track a
`downloaded` flag or keep last-N finished).

### M9. Frame band (auto) slider misrepresents its value on large boxes and uses the wrong multiplier for wreath
`frontend/src/ui/FrameControls.tsx:117` — auto band = 0.12·min(w,h) can reach
9.6 mm on an 80 mm box while the slider max is hardcoded 6000 (thumb pins at
the edge); and the flat 0.12 never branches on algorithm although the backend
uses 0.13 for wreath (~8 % display error). **Fix:** derive max from live face
dims; branch the multiplier on `f.algorithm`.

### M10. Seam 3D positions and hinge placement exist only in the frontend, unpinned by the golden fixture
`frontend/src/assembly.ts:248` — backend `seam_list` has no 3D coordinates;
`seamSegments`/`platePlacements`/`hingeLayout` positions are checked only by
hardcoded frontend unit tests, not the cross-implementation fixture. **Fix:**
add the position formulas to `assembly.py` and pin them in
`gen_assembly_golden.py` + both suites — or explicitly scope them as
frontend-only in the module doc so the contract comment doesn't overclaim.

## Fidelity roadmap — solder, hinges, and effects honesty

Ranked; items marked ★ also close confirmed defects at the root.

**Solder & hinge hardware**

- **R1 ★ (medium)** True wetting-meniscus bead cross-section, oriented by the
  seam's outward bisector: extend `SeamSegment` with `outward_um` +
  `dihedral_deg` (both contract sides + golden regen), replace the circular
  section with flat wetted legs + a contact-angle arc (30–45° for tin on
  tinned copper), orient via an orthonormal basis. Root-fixes M1 + L4.
- **R2 (small)** Drive wetted width from the assembly foil overlap
  (`overlapUm(spec)`) and reinterpret `bead_um` as proud crown height, clamped
  — makes the tape-width control visibly matter and keeps beads sane at every
  box size.
- **R3 (small)** Hinge knuckles as real tubes with a bore (one shared
  LatheGeometry) + peened pin caps at the rod ends — makes `rod_od_um`
  visible; update the visualSignatures census counts.
- **R4 (small)** Attachment fillets where each knuckle meets its plate
  (body → back wall, lid → lid edge, parented like the knuckles) — the
  floating hinge is the biggest "CAD assembly" tell on the box.
- **R5 (medium)** Contact AO at seams and under the hinge: aoMap from the
  existing `makeStripHeatColor` distance field + darkened vertex ring where
  the meniscus meets glass. Static, geometric, honest.
- **R6 (medium)** Reflow micro-detail: transverse chill ripples (the
  signature of a hand-run bead) + a real normal map instead of bumpMap, which
  washes out at grazing angles.
- **R7 (small)** Give solder its own alloy tint/microsurface distinct from the
  tape finish (currently identical hue); wires up the dead `solderRough`.
- **R8 ★ (medium)** Rebuild tin rims as thin offset shells derived from the
  cut list (root-fixes M4, gives tinning real UVs/maps).
- **R9 (small)** Seam/hinge inspection overlay from `seamSegments` +
  `hingeLayout`, plus a raycast assertion (outside hits bead, inside doesn't)
  in the existing e2e harness — makes this whole defect class self-evident.

**Optical / effects honesty** (all in-canvas per contract — the audit found
no DOM/CSS effect fakes and no time-driven effect animation; the defects are
physics accuracy, not honesty violations, except H4/H5 which break the
second-surface story from inside the box)

- **R10 (medium)** Bind the PMREM studio environment into plate.frag — the
  gold plates currently see one light out of five and go flat off the key
  direction while the copper foil beside them reflects the room.
- **R11 (medium)** Replace Schlick with exact conductor Fresnel from
  Johnson & Christy Au n,k at three wavelengths — Schlick's error shape (blue
  channel, 40–70°) sits exactly on the orbits the tool encourages.
- **R12 (medium)** Sellmeier-dispersive substrate index for
  wavelength-resolved terms (keep the plane gap on scalar photopic n) —
  plumbing for the physically-correct diffraction accent (M6).
- **R13 (small)** Delete the dead `runMoireInteractive`/`runStereoLenticular`
  recipes + uViewA/uViewB samplers (unreachable, silently miscalibrated since
  the linear-light change).
- **R14 (small)** @effects assertion that renders the *interior* of the open
  box — the blind spot that let H4 survive.
